**SHORT DESCRIPTION**

Hunt beavers. Collect pelts. Hire hands. Try not to get beaten up.

**BEAVER HUNTERS OF SPLORR!!**

Hunt beavers in order to collect beaver pelts. That is the game.

**HOW IT WORKS**

- Send the hunter out. After a short trip, the hunter comes back with a pelt.
- Spend pelts on hired hands. They make trips on their own, and the better ones haul more.
- Every trip wears on the beavers of the area. You cannot see how much. The beavers are named, they keep count, and they remember you.
- When an area has had enough, the beavers gang up. Yer hands take the beating first, the best ones first. If nobody is left to stand between you and them, you lose yer pelts and yer hands, and the beaver who led the charge never forgets it.
- Move on to a new area and the old one cools off. Go back later and it is exactly as you left it, scars and all.
- The game saves in yer browser and keeps running while the tab is closed, for up to an hour. A beating can find you while you are away.

There is no ending. There is a pile of pelts, and the question of how long it stays yours.

**CONTROLS**

- **H** or **Space**: hunt. **T**: train the hunter to bring back more. **S**: hire a Soft Touch. **R**: hire a Rough Hand. **M**: move on. **Left** and **Right**: visit a remembered area. **Esc**: title.
- Everything is also clickable and tappable.
