# Trip text: draft for the user to edit

Candidates only. Cut, rewrite and add freely; once settled, the chosen lines replace the placeholders in
`src/game/data.odin` (`TRIP_LINES`, `NAMED_FRAGMENTS`, `SCARRED_FRAGMENTS`). Tone: played straight, suggestive not
explicit. "yer" only ever means "your" or "you're"; "you" stays "you".

## Plain lines (shown on their own)

1. You check the traps. Something has been at the bait.
2. You spend a long morning looking for a good dam.
3. Nothing beats a fresh pelt on a cold day.
4. You work the bank until it is wet through.
5. You get a firm grip and pull.
6. A good pelt is soft and thick and worth the wait.
7. You never enter the lodge uninvited. Mostly.
8. You open the lodge up and find it well furnished.
9. It was a bigger one than you expected.
10. You hold it to the light and admire the fur.
11. You go down to the water to see what is out there.
12. Slow work. You like to take yer time.
